Challenge

BMW/MINI needed to streamline global creative production across 27+ markets while maintaining brand consistency. The scale of operations demanded a system capable of handling thousands of assets simultaneously, with rapid localization and deployment across diverse platforms and regions.

Solution

We built a Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO) system — an automated asset generation pipeline enabling real-time localization, brand compliance validation, and multi-platform deployment. The system supports over 300M+ dynamic variable combinations, ensuring every market receives on-brand, locally relevant creative at scale.
